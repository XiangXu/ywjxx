## 怎么使用 How to use

- **语言 Language**: 点工具栏里的 `中 / EN` 切换中文和英文，选择会被记住。
  Switch between Chinese and English with `中 / EN` in the toolbar. Your choice is remembered.
- **搜索 Search**: 按 `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` 搜索标题和全文。
  Press `⌘K` / `Ctrl K` to search titles and full text.
- **标签 Tags**: 点任意笔记上的标签，查看所有带这个标签的笔记。
  Click a tag on any note to see every note that shares it.
- **反向链接 Backlinks**: 每篇笔记的末尾列出了链接到它的笔记。
  The end of each note lists the notes that link to it.
